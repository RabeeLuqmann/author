About the Author

Rabeè Luqmann is a German-Jordanian author with a passion for exploring the complexities of love, desire, survival, and the human condition across cultures. His works weave vivid cultural tapestries and powerful storytelling, whether unraveling the tensions between duty and passion or chronicling the fight for freedom.
