A little about the author

Stories shaped by many worlds

Rabeè Luqmann is a German-Jordanian author whose work explores love, desire and the human condition across cultures. His stories move between places and perspectives, bringing together questions of identity, belonging, freedom and the choices that define us.

“Every life carries a story. Some stories cross borders; others change the way we see the world.”
